The opportunity

Ground-up, 8-story, 85 ft mixed-use condominium on South 2nd Street in Williamsburg's Southside: 9 residences above two floors of commercial space.

  • 246 South 2nd's own floor area in R6 with qualifying affordable housing, with the 5% Zone Green credit. No air rights or certificates needed.
  • Residential: 6,879 sellable sf in 9 homes, one of them an affordable homeownership unit. Commercial: 1,717 sf at grade, a 2,616 sf 2nd floor and 1,857 sf below grade.
  • Floor 8: a full-floor penthouse with a setback terrace and a 1,505 sf private roof. Common terrace on floor 3.
  • All homes presell. All-electric, ultra-low-energy; 20-month build.

Capital

$4.40M of equity funds the land and pre-development. A construction loan of up to $9.30M (70% of cost, 9.5%) funds the balance; peak draw $8.63M.
